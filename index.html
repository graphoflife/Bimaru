How to play

Find every ship hidden in the sea.

  1. 1
    The fleet above the board is hidden in the grid. Ships lie straight, horizontally or vertically.
  2. 2
    The numbers tell you how many ship segments are in each row and column.
  3. 3
    Ships never touch each other, not even at the corners.
  4. 4
    Some pieces are given. A rounded end means the ship continues the other way; a square piece is a middle part.
  5. 5
    Tap a cell to cycle water → ship → empty. Drag to paint many cells. Tap a finished number to fill the rest of its line with water.
  6. 6
    Every puzzle is solvable by logic alone, never by guessing. Stuck? Hint shows the next move and explains why it must be so.

Keyboard: ←↑→↓ move · Space cycle · W water · S ship · ⌫ clear · Ctrl Z undo · H hint. Right-click cycles backwards.
